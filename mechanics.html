Game Mechanics

How the game itself is played, round by round.

Objective

Two teams compete to guess the most popular answers to survey questions. The team with the most points at the end wins.

Rounds

Each round presents one question with up to 8 possible answers, each worth a different number of points based on how popular that answer was. Higher-ranked answers are worth more points.

Revealing Answers

When a team gives a correct guess, the host flips the matching card to reveal the answer and its point value. Revealed points add up in the center score display as the round goes on.

Wrong Answers

A wrong guess adds a strike. Three strikes means the round is up for grabs — the opposing team gets a chance to steal all the accumulated points by naming one remaining unrevealed answer.

Awarding Points

Once a round is settled — either a team fully controls the board or successfully steals — the host awards the accumulated points to that team. The points are added to the team's running total, and the accumulated round score resets to zero for the next round.

Winning

After all rounds have been played, the team with the higher total score wins the game.
